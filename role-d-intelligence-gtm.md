# Role D — Intelligence + GTM Glue

**Project:** A voiceprint tool that self-selects champions and writes their outreach in Lightfern. You own the brains (the prompts), the seed research, the live sponsor ops, and the pitch.
*(Full context: the master build brief.)*

## Your mission
The product's *quality* lives in your prompts, and the *win* lives in your pitch and sponsor ops. Your work is front-loaded (prompts + research feed A and B) and back-loaded (ops + pitch) — in the middle, float to unblock whoever's stuck.

## What you own, in order

### 1. The voiceprint prompt → hand to A
Input: pasted writing. Output: a structured **portrait** (rhythm, tone, signature moves, lexical character, structure) + a **voice archetype** label + a **one-line signature**.
- **Mirror, not judge.** Descriptive, flattering-but-true. Never score, never say "slop"/"generic," never accuse of using AI. Find something interesting in *every* voice — including plain, workmanlike writing. This is the rule that protects the tool.
- Iterate on real samples — paste several different writing styles and check the portrait feels accurate *and* flattering.

### 2. The champion-scoring prompt → hand to B
Input: a lead row (writing + role + handle). Output: **0–100 + a short breakdown** across mission fit / email-volume (role) / network effect (handle). (This one *is* allowed to rank — it's the back office, not the user-facing tool.)

### 3. Champion hand-seed research → hand to B
10–15 **real** champions: craft/comms Substack writers, GTM thought leaders, taste-forward VCs, AI educators. For each: name, role, handle, a link, one line of why. Draft with Claude/ChatGPT, then **verify each one is real** before handing over. Don't let this balloon — 10–15 solid, verified, then stop.

### 4. Sponsor live-ops (do these on the day — this is how we score on sponsors)
- **Lightfern:** install it, **compose 2–3 champion outreach emails in it**, in voice, referencing the champion's actual work. Screen-record it. This is the dogfood.
- **Zero:** send one of those emails through the **Zero client (0.email)** — connect a Gmail, send live. If it won't connect cleanly in a few minutes, cut it (don't fake it).

### 5. Seed the room + pitch
- Get hackathon attendees using the tool so the pipeline fills before demo.
- Own the demo script (master brief has a draft) and likely present.

## Your timeline
- **0:00–0:30** — v1 voiceprint prompt + start champion research
- **0:30–3:00** — tune both prompts (hand to A/B), finish research, float to help
- **3:00–4:30** — Lightfern outreach + tune scoring with B
- **4:30–5:15** — lock the pitch
- **5:15–6:00** — seed the room + dry-run

## Gotcha
Your prompts are the difference between a tool that feels magic and one that feels generic. Spend real time tuning them on varied inputs — it's the highest-leverage hour of the day.
