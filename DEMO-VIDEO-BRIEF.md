## What it actually does (the part you film)

You paste anything you've written — a few sentences of an email, a post, a note.
Voiceprint reflects it back as a flattering, specific portrait:

- a **voice archetype** ("You're The Striker") with a one-line tagline,
- a **signature line** — your voice distilled into one quotable sentence,
- a short **portrait** (rhythm, tone, signature moves, lexical character, structure),
- a **waveform** of your sentence rhythm,
- a **shareable card** (a 1080×1080 image) you'd actually want to post.

**The one rule — say it in the video if you can:** it's a **mirror, not a judge.**
It *describes* how you write; it never scores, grades, or calls writing "generic"
or "AI." Everyone's voice is interesting. That's the whole emotional hook — people
paste their own writing and feel *seen*, not graded.

The eight archetypes: **The Striker, The Essayist, The Cartographer, The
Conversationalist, The Storyteller, The Minimalist, The Craftsman, The Spark.**

## Why it exists (the GTM story — the "ideally")

This is a go-to-market engine disguised as a fun toy. The full vision:

1. **The hook (the viral front door).** Voiceprint is irresistible to share — it
   flatters you and gives you a shareable identity ("I'm The Storyteller"). People
   who care about *how they write* are exactly Lightfern's ideal customers.
2. **The capture.** To unlock the full portrait, you drop your email (role +
   LinkedIn optional). Now Lightfern knows who you are and has a real sample of
   your voice.
3. **The champion engine (behind the scenes).** Every person who uses the tool
   quietly becomes a row in a **champion table** — scored on how strong a
   *champion* they'd be for Lightfern: do they care about voice and write a lot
   (mission fit), do they send high-volume outbound (role), and do they have reach
   (network)? The best are flagged for outreach. **The user never sees a score —
   the tool stays a mirror; the ranking is ours.**
4. **The payoff.** Lightfern then reaches out to the best-fit people **in their
   own voice** — proving the product in the very first email. The tool that read
   your voice becomes the product that keeps it.

**One sentence:** *a viral mirror that turns "what's my writing voice?" into a
ranked list of the people most likely to love an AI tool that protects their
voice — and reaches them in it.*

## Suggested 60–90s storyboard

1. **Hook (0–8s).** Screen: the tool's headline. VO: *"What does your writing
   actually sound like?"*
2. **Paste (8–18s).** Paste a chunk of writing with personality (punchy, opinionated
   — see sample below). Hit the button.
3. **The teaser (18–25s).** A glimpse of the archetype appears — withhold the full
   thing. VO: *"It reads you back to yourself."*
4. **The gate (25–32s).** Enter an email to unlock. VO: *"Drop your email to see the
   whole portrait."* (Role + LinkedIn are optional — keep it fast.)
5. **The reveal (32–50s).** The full portrait animates in: archetype, signature
   line, the four blocks, the waveform, the share card. Let it breathe — this is the
   money shot. VO: *"Your archetype. Your signature line. A card you'll want to share."*
6. **The twist (50–70s).** Cut to **/champions**. VO: *"And every person who does
   this becomes a ranked lead for Lightfern — scored on who'd love it most. They
   only ever see a mirror. We see a go-to-market engine."*
7. **The close (70–85s).** Lightfern logo / line. VO: *"Lightfern keeps your voice
   in every email. Voiceprint is how it finds the people who care — and reaches them
   in theirs."*
