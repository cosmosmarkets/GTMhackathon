What "slop" actually is

Slop is the even, padded, faintly corporate text you can spot from across the room — "I hope this email finds you well," three adjectives where one would do, every paragraph the same length. It's fluent and it's empty, because it's written toward the average of everything a model has read. No edges, no taste, no you.

For founders, that's a real cost. Your writing is how people decide whether to trust you, reply to you, work with you. Generic writing quietly tells them you didn't care enough to sound like yourself.

Why most AI writing tools produce it

They optimise for "correct and safe," which converges on the same bland centre for everyone. A better prompt doesn't escape it — you're still pulling from an average. The only real exit is a tool anchored to a specific voice.
