Why your voice disappears with most AI

The moment you ask a general AI to "write this for me," it reaches for the most average phrasing it knows. That's not a bug — it's what a model trained on everyone's text is built to do. Your edges get sanded off: the aside you'd have made, the short sentence you'd have landed on, the word only you use. What's left is fluent and forgettable.

The method: anchor the AI to you

Keeping your voice isn't about a cleverer prompt. It's about giving the AI you to work from. Three principles:

  • Show, don't describe. Paste real examples of your writing — the AI matches patterns far better than it follows "write casually."
  • Edit toward yourself, not toward "correct." Keep the fragments, the dashes, the rhythm that's yours.
  • Use a voice-aware tool so you don't redo this every time. That's the part Lightfern automates.
