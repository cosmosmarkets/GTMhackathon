# Demo script + sponsor runbook (Role D)

The win lives in the pitch and the sponsor ops. Keep the live demo to ~3 minutes.

## The story (say it like this)

> Everyone's outreach now sounds the same — because everyone's using the same AI.
> Lightfern is the opposite: AI that sounds like *you*. To prove it, we built a
> tool that reads your writing back to you.

**The turn:** the tool isn't just a toy — **the people who use it self-select into
our champion list.** They paste their writing, they tell us their role and handle,
and that's exactly the signal we need to know who to reach. The viral front door
*is* the lead-gen engine.

## Live demo flow (3 min)

1. **Open the tool** (voiceprint-lightfern.vercel.app) on a phone. Paste a judge's
   or attendee's writing (or the built-in sample). Hit "Get my voiceprint."
2. **Teaser → gate.** A glimpse appears, then the email/role/handle gate. Point out:
   *"It's a mirror, not a judge — it never scores you. It flatters, accurately."*
3. **Full reveal.** Archetype + signature + shareable card. *"People screenshot this.
   That's the loop."*
4. **Flip to the back office.** Show the champion table (B): every submission, scored
   0–100 for champion-fit, ranked, top N flagged. *"A toy on the front; a pipeline
   on the back."*
5. **Close on Lightfern.** *"And here's the outreach — written in their voice, in
   Lightfern."* Show the dogfood email (below).

## Sponsor live-ops — DO THESE ON THE DAY (this is how we score on sponsors)

- [ ] **Lightfern (dogfood):** install it, **compose 2–3 champion outreach emails in
      it**, in the champion's own voice, referencing their actual work. Screen-record it.
      Pull the champions from `data/champions.seed.json`.
- [ ] **Zero (0.email):** send ONE of those emails through the Zero client — connect a
      Gmail, send live. If it won't connect cleanly in a few minutes, cut it (don't fake).
- [ ] **Scaille (GrowthGPT):** run the target queries + "Lightfern" through Scaille's
      free visibility tool; screenshot what it surfaces. (Role C owns the GEO pages this
      feeds — coordinate.) This is the "Scaille is *used*, not cited" moment.

## Seed the room

Before judging, get attendees to run the tool so the pipeline fills with real,
impressive rows. The hand-seeded champions guarantee the table looks strong even
if foot traffic is light.

- *"The tool is a mirror, not a judge — that's the whole reason people share it."*
- *"Three signals in one paste: how they write, what they do, who they reach."*
